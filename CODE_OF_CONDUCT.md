# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1. The short version is below; the linked text is the one that applies.

## The commitment

Everyone who opens an issue, reviews a pull request or comments here can expect to be
treated with respect, whatever their experience, background, identity or the tools they
prefer. Disagree about code and templates as much as you like. Do not make it personal.

## What that looks like

- Assume the other person is trying to help, and say what you would change and why.
- Accept a review comment about your template as a comment about the template.
- Credit other people's ideas, and never paste someone else's workflow in as your own
  (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md), "What does not belong here").
- Keep real people's data out of issues as well as out of fixtures. Redact emails,
  phone numbers, sheet ids and webhook URLs before you paste an execution log.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, slurs or sexualised language and imagery.
- Personal or political attacks, and trolling.
- Publishing someone's private information, such as a home address or an email
  address, without their permission.
- Continuing to contact someone after they have asked you to stop.

## Scope

This applies in the repository's issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews, and
anywhere you represent the project in public.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately through a
[GitHub security advisory](https://github.com/AthAsh007/automation-workflows-pipelines/security/advisories/new)
or by email to the address on the owner's [GitHub profile](https://github.com/AthAsh007).
Reports are read by the maintainer only and kept confidential. Expect a reply within a week.

## Enforcement

The maintainer decides what happens after a report, from a private note to a
permanent ban, following the
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines)
in the Contributor Covenant.
